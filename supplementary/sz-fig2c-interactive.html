The recurrent genes in each study

Controls and legend
Each column is one of the 383 recurrent genes and each row one study. Clustering puts studies with the most similar results next to each other (the genes each lists up or down, and the fold changes where held), and likewise for genes across studies. Click a gene (its column or its name) to sort the studies by it, or click a study name (or shift-click a cell) to sort the genes by it; click again to reverse. Click a label on the left of the strips to sort the genes by that strip. Hover over a cell, strip or gene for details. Scroll sideways for more genes.
